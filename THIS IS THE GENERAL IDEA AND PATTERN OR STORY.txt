THE STORY WE ARE TELLING - PRESENTATION SCRIPT
================================================

Open the presentation with the question
   ->  "How do NHS elective care waiting times vary across England's regions?
        Is this a funding and staffing issue, or is something else driving
        these disparities?"

Vis 1 -- Set the scene. Show the decade-long failure.
   ->  "The NHS 18-week target has not been met since 2017. The decline
        started before COVID. The system has not recovered."

Vis 2 -- Show that this isn't uniform across the country.
   ->  "Pre-COVID every region performed similarly. After 2020 they fanned
        out. Same NHS, same target, same shock, but very different outcomes."

Vis 3 -- Bring it to life. Show the postcode lottery emerging.
   ->  "Watch how the country starts uniformly green. After 2020 the
        south-east turns red and stays red. The geography of failure has
        emerged."

Vis 4 -- Confront the obvious assumption.
   ->  "The obvious explanation is underfunding and understaffing. But the
        NHS workforce has grown by over a third in a decade. So if it isn't
        that, what is it?"

Vis 5 -- Reveal the actual driver.
   ->  "It is demand. The waiting list more than doubled. Demand grew over
        four times faster than the workforce. We are recovering, but only
        slowly."

Vis 6 -- Make it personal.
   ->  "Find your trust. Type the name. See what is happening at your local
        hospital. This is what you are walking into."

Closing line
   ->  "It is not staffing. It is demand. And recovery has only just started."
